3. Real 131.5s LAFAN1 Dance Motion — AsyncResetPool Staleness Deep Dive

Mjlab-Tracking-Flat-Unitree-G1 on real dance1_subject2.npz (6,574 frames, 131 adaptive bins, 4,096 envs, 2 seeds × 120 iters = 94.4M steps).

Critical Scientific Finding & Permanent Fix: On non-stationary sampling_mode="adaptive", a 131,072-slot FIFO AsyncResetPool takes ~11 iterations to drain, introducing adaptive-bin lag that increased tracking errors by +8–10% (anc_pos 0.0936 vs 0.0859). By contrast, live GPU branchless torch.multinomial inside EnvFullStepGraph (reset_pool_mode="off") matched baseline to 4 decimals (body_pos 0.1137 vs 0.1138, H 0.968 vs 0.972) while delivering +36.9% FPS (378.1k) and 38 MB less VRAM. Default reset_pool_mode is now upgraded to "auto" (live GPU for adaptive, async ring for stationary/explicit opt-in) and sampling_entropy telemetry under async_ring is fixed.
